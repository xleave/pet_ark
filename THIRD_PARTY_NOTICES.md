The checked-in registry records source identifiers and derived visual descriptions. Public source pages were retrieved on 2026-08-12:

- Official Hypergryph operator archive: <https://ak.hypergryph.com/archive/dynamicCompile>
- PRTS playable-operator index: <https://prts.wiki/w/干员一览>

- Aceship/Arknight-Images avatar index: <https://github.com/Aceship/Arknight-Images/tree/master/avatars>

The latter three sources provide machine indexing or temporary visual-analysis inputs where the official archive is not machine-readable enough for roster compilation. Source portraits are not checked in, copied into output, or distributed by this repository. The compiler extracts only small color and silhouette measurements; all pet pixels are rendered from project-owned SVG/vector code.
